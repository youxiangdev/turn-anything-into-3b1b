# Overview: Two parcel-locker networks

## What this is
An invented eight-page paper that compares how two parcel-locker networks, Lockr and BoxPost,
handle a delivery from the courier's scan to the closed door.

## Why it matters
Both networks solve the same problem with opposite timing, which makes the trade-off easy to see.

## How the source is organised
Pages 1 to 2 introduce the networks, 3 to 7 walk through a delivery, page 8 concludes.

## Who and what it covers
Lockr and BoxPost.

## Caveats and limits
The paper's figure for BoxPost's reservation length is out of date (see P2.7).

## Questions for the scope stage
